# CareerSync 🚀

**CareerSync** is a career development platform designed to help students and job seekers prepare for employment and navigate the job search process more effectively. The platform brings together job and internship opportunities, resume building, and ATS-based resume analysis in one place.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Smart Job Search:** Search and filter job and internship opportunities based on job title, location, and job type.
- **ATS-Based Resume Checker:** Analyze resumes for relevant keywords, formatting, and compatibility with Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).
- **Resume Builder:** Create professional, structured resumes to showcase skills, qualifications, and experience.
- **Career Preparation:** Help students and job seekers improve their resumes and prepare for employment opportunities.
- **Centralized Platform:** Access essential job search and resume preparation tools through a single platform.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of CareerSync is to simplify the career preparation process by bringing essential job search and resume-building tools together. It aims to help students and job seekers identify relevant opportunities, improve their resumes, and make more informed career decisions.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** React.js, Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS *(update according to the actual implementation)*
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
- **Database:** PostgreSQL

## 👥 Target Users

- College students seeking internships and placement opportunities
- Fresh graduates preparing to enter the job market
- Job seekers looking for relevant employment opportunities
